# Pagpag: The Hunger Games — Phase 2: World Map and Locations

> **Revision 2 (2026-10-21) changes this phase.** Stats now run 0–100 with 100 = best ("Fatigue" is now "Energy"), the family and pantry exist, every job is a minigame, and the location tables gained family food and new home actions. **Scope:** a clickable world map, travel, a screen for each location with its own actions, the HUD, and the basic time and survival loop. The loop is what makes the two difficulty modes actually play differently.

## World scene layout

- **HUD** (top): portrait, name, difficulty, day and period, clock, current location, ₱, and Health / Hunger / Fatigue bars (they pulse red when critical), plus a Menu button that saves and returns to the main menu.
- **Map view**: the district art with 12 clickable pins. Each pin shows its live status: green = you are here, gold = open, grey = closed, red = locked. The map tint changes with the time of day.
- **Location view**: a close-up of that part of the map, the place's description and hours, the player character, and action cards showing cost, time and effects. An action you can't do right now shows the reason.
- **Sidebar**: the full location list plus a panel for the selected place with a thumbnail, hours, an unlock hint, and the travel buttons (**Walk**, free, or **Jeepney**, ₱13 and faster on long trips), or **Enter** / **Return to Map**.
- **Message log**: results, warnings and unlocks.
- **Intro overlay**: the character's story pages, then Day 1 and How to Survive. Shown once per run.
- **Game over overlay**: cause of death and run statistics. The save is deleted (hardcore).

## Survival rules and difficulty

Base rates per in-game hour (`GameConfig`):

| | Awake | Asleep |
|---|---|---|
| Hunger | +4 | +2 |
| Fatigue | +2.5 | −12.5 |

- **Tagalog (Normal)** uses these rates as they are. **Bisaya (Hard)** multiplies every hunger and fatigue gain, and the health damage from starving or exhaustion, by 1.6. Nothing else changes.
- Health drains at hunger ≥ 85 or fatigue ≥ 90 (slowly) and at 100 (fast). It recovers slowly while you are fed and rested. Health 0 means death, which is permanent.
- Work is refused at fatigue ≥ 85 or hunger ≥ 90.
- Warnings are posted at hunger 75, fatigue 80 and health 35.

**Economy with ₱500:** a day of food costs roughly ₱60–90 on Normal and ₱100–150 on Hard. One shift pays ₱150–₱480. ₱500 buys about 4–5 days on Normal, or 3 on Hard, before you must earn. Rent and other living costs come in Phase 8.

## Testing

**Automated** (Window ▸ General ▸ Test Runner ▸ EditMode ▸ Run All): **39 tests**, all passing. The world-related tests cover:
- the difficulty gap (Hard gains hunger and fatigue more than 1.5× as fast, dies sooner, and earns the same wages);
- starvation death and its recorded cause;
- sleep;
- starting unlocks, walking vs jeepney costs, the locked Port and Train Station;
- closed hours, eating, affordability;
- Dong- and Inday-specific jobs and the balance between them;
- the exhaustion limit on work.

**Manual:**
1. Start a run. After the intro you are inside **Your Room**, with 4 actions (one of them is your own cooking).
2. Click **Return to Map**. The pins show live status and **Your Room** is green.
3. Click **Public Market**. The sidebar shows walk 30 min (free) or jeepney 15 min (₱13). Take the jeepney; ₱ drops by 13 and you arrive inside the market.
4. As Inday, do **Help Aling Nena** (6–11 AM). As Dong, try the market's **Haul crates** or the construction **Morning shift** (6–9 AM). Money goes up, time passes, the bars rise, and **Fish Market unlocked** appears.
5. The Pagpag Stall shows "Closed · opens 4 PM" in the morning.
6. Go home and **Sleep**. Fatigue drops and the clock advances 8 hours.
7. Play the same day as Hard and as Normal and compare the Hunger and Fatigue bars.
8. Click **Menu**, then **Continue**. You resume exactly where you were, and the intro is not shown again.
